5. **Lived Payoff:** Cut to the same homeowner relaxing calmly on the newly arranged couch, with family members collaborating seamlessly inside the app to finalize decor choices without arguing.

### Three Concrete Transferable Devices
1. **Floating Spatial UI Overlays:** Use 3D-tracked floating UI cards and measurement tags anchored to physical room surfaces during live-action pans to visualize spatial data without cluttering the screen.
2. **The "Chaos-to-Clarity" Montage:** Contrast an overwhelming visual storm of disconnected design ideas (swatches, screenshots, PDFs) against a single unified spatial dashboard.
3. **Dry Comedic Intercuts:** Pair technical spatial scanning steps with deadpan talking-head testimonials from real users acknowledging common home-renovation pain points.
