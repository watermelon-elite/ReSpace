# Keep room structure fixed while editing furniture, decor, and finishes

ReSpace permanently models one room at a time on LiDAR-equipped phones. Only freestanding furniture and decor move; walls and built-in cabinets, counters, doors and windows stay fixed, while wallpaper, tile, and other finishes change through textures/materials. Separate movable item identities and placement operations from fixed room surfaces and finish operations, preserving architectural geometry and openings during redesign. This deliberately excludes structural redesign and avoids treating a finish replacement as a wall mesh replacement.
