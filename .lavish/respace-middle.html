The four rising paper cards

Collected article-opening screenshots from the actual PDFs (the green-office study skips its repository cover sheet). Two psychology papers and two functional-layout papers already referenced by the planner’s advisory context. These are source visuals; the proposed ReSpace applications below are inferences.

Room identity

Gosling et al. · 2002
A Room With a Cue

On-screen label: “Personal expression.” Ask what deserves display. Observational room cues do not establish that decoration changes personality.

Experience of workspaces

Nieuwenhuis et al. · 2014
The Relative Benefits of Green Versus Lean Office Space

On-screen label: “Workspace experience.” Offer optional greenery. Commercial-office experiments do not predict a focus or productivity gain in this dorm.

Functional arrangement

Merrell et al. · 2011
Interactive Furniture Layout Using Interior Design Guidelines

On-screen label: “Conversation + clearance.” Show seating orientation or a clear path. Its layout priors are guidance, not universal guarantees.

Design around activities

Fisher et al. · 2015
Activity-centric Scene Synthesis for Functional 3D Scene Modeling

On-screen label: “Space for actual activities.” Make the desk and chair work together. Simplified activity models do not validate all lived-room uses.

Film treatment. Let the four cards occupy the lower half of the frame, then shrink them into one research node. A title and a short topic label are enough. Keep the original papers intact; any emphasis or colored annotation is visibly an editorial overlay.

What was missing, and what needs tightening

Keep + edit + Undo earn screen time

These give the person control before and after generation. Keep is already in this draft; the smaller-lamp edit and a brief Undo complete the demonstration. Show the total respecting the approved budget.

The solver is the key technical addition

Your outline explained scanning and Gemini but skipped the bridge to actual placement. Gemini proposes spatial relationships; the solver calculates positions and checks constraints. This explains physical fit more clearly than “the AI makes a 3D room.”

Keep the research claim specific

“Research-informed” matches the advisory context loaded into planning, ranking, edits and spatial prompts. “Psychology-backed optimal room” goes beyond that evidence. Tie a cited idea to a visible design choice; use no guaranteed wellbeing or productivity claim.

Cache savings need measurement

Current discovery combines stored candidates with fresh SerpAPI searches. Fresh eligible evidence can be reused; stale or unsuitable offers trigger checks. It is not a browser visit on every cache hit. “Reuse verified details” is supported; dramatic cost reductions and consistently quick checks are unmeasured.

Generated comparisons are a creative extension

The governing product flow specifies verified listing images for concrete Product questions. Two generated atmosphere references can illustrate your intended taste interaction, but require implementation work. Label them as style references so they do not imply purchasable products.

Distinguish the three kinds of 3D work

RoomPlan captures room geometry. Product meshes/stand-ins represent furnishings; SPAR3D is an offline reconstruction path for selected catalog products. The solver places them. A photorealistic image is a separate proposed presentation layer and must preserve the validated arrangement.

Sharing is prepared, then the user publishes

The current shopping exporter uses labeled placeholder-room images plus saved links and totals. Instagram uses the native share sheet and a separately copied caption; it is not automatic publication. The completed-product room-render share is your creative target, not evidence of today’s exporter output.

Business spaces and community need proportion

The existing film already assumes completed-product café/office use cases; keep them as requested. The app specification currently defines a personal-room experience. Community discovery from the old middle is the one feature omitted here: at most add a two-second feed insert beside sharing if it remains part of the creative scope.

My editorial choice: keep the cache/database explanation to a short graphic inside the product beat. The full reveal and Mom pause need breathing room. Saved Rooms, offline recovery, cancellation and every alternative-buying control can stay outside the spoken launch script.
