# ReSpace launch-film candidate ledger

**120 candidates screened → 20 selected.** Research date: 17 October 2026 (Los Angeles). This is a record of source/metadata screening, not a claim that 120 films were watched. The first 105 have individual film, brand or creator links; the final 15 are explicitly provisional X discovery leads. Six separately titled IKEA works share one creator portfolio page. Distinct edits/reposts are not counted again.

Selection is editorial judgment about this ReSpace brief. “Reserve” means useful but displaced by a stronger or better-evidenced reference, not a poor film. Public counts are snapshots, may include paid distribution, and do not measure retention or conversion. X post views are not comparable to YouTube video views.

[Read the ranked shortlist and analysis](shortlist.md). [Structured ledger](candidates.json). [Automated footage previews](footage-previews.md).
