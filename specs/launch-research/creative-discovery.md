# Creative launch-film discovery

Observed: October 17, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). 38 distinct films selected from public brand/studio sources. This is discovery and screening, not a shot-by-shot viewing report. No film in this file was watched end to end. Rationale statements are research hypotheses unless explicitly attributed to a primary publisher.

The list intentionally mixes product launches, product explainers and brand films. A brand film can be a stronger worldview/identity reference than a literal launch. It should not be mislabeled as a launch, or as proven viral.

The public [Sandwich catalogue](https://sandwich.co/wp-json/custom/v1/all-work) returned 587 records. Twenty-seven relevant distinct films were selected; alternate-length edits, behind-the-scenes pieces and reels were excluded. Their individual pages and public Wistia metadata were fetched and matched by video ID. This does not mean 587 videos were watched or that every catalogue record was a viable candidate. Eleven other brand/creator sources broadened the styles.

YouTube counts below are search-index snapshots, not current counters or proof of organic reach. Wistia/Vimeo views were unavailable. Missing views are unknown, never zero. The discovery cannot establish a highest-viewed ranking across the whole internet.

## Eight to prioritize for actual viewing

1. **[reMarkable — Get your brain back](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OcdxW2XF0v8)**. Priority: the official description opens with a societal problem—information overload and lost focus—and asks whether technology is changing us. Closest documented analogue to a human environment/personality thesis leading toward a product.
2. **[Descript — Damn, Jenny!](https://sandwich.co/work/descript-video/)**. Priority: translate an unfamiliar AI capability into a lively, understandable story. Sandwich explicitly calls it fast, colorful, fun, detailed, and its best-performing Descript spot; no numerical performance metric is supplied.
3. **[Notion — Meet Notion Mail](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54l-1NfXnX8)**. Priority: personalization provides a direct rhetorical bridge to spatial personality. The launch proposition is an inbox that thinks like its user; study how identity is connected to an interface.
4. **[Headspace — Say Hello to Headspace](https://vimeo.com/226652947)**. Priority: the director explicitly says this animation introduces both the benefits of mindfulness and how the new app works. Good evidence of the broad human idea → app bridge the user wants.
5. **[Dropbox — Creative Freedom](https://vimeo.com/142497378)**. Priority: director Johnny Kelly describes a 60-second film illustrating what creative freedom enables, assembled from more than 100 collaborators. Strong evidence for a broad worldview-led brand film and visual variety.
6. **[Daylight — Not Like The Others](https://sandwich.co/work/not-like-the-others/)**. Priority candidate for technology serving human attention. Primary credits establish a professional live-action production with voiceover, graphics and VFX; exact thesis and pacing still need viewing.
7. **[Panic — Say Hello to Playdate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HdF3CnFvxg4)**. Priority: physical personality and delight for an unfamiliar product. Official brand description promises a unique handheld; studio confirms animation, live-action and effects production.
8. **[Apple — Perspective](https://www.apple.com/perspective/)**. Candidate for a design worldview opening and spatial visual storytelling. Brand film, not a startup launch; exact shot and transition observations require viewing.

For the eight, record first 3 seconds, first product appearance, big-picture-to-product transition, every demonstrable proof moment, duration of interface-only shots, sound/motion relationship, and the ending/CTA. Do not assert these details from thumbnails or transcripts alone.
