# ReSpace launch-film discovery: spatial, home and design references

Research snapshot: 2026-10-17 (America/Los_Angeles). **52 distinct film candidates. This is discovery and screening, not a completed footage analysis.** No film in this file was watched in full or frame-analyzed during this pass.

The requested creative territory is a professional launch film that starts with identity and the importance of our surroundings, then makes ReSpace's room scanning and real-product redesign the natural resolution. The pool intentionally includes direct product competitors, home and furniture campaigns, consumer technology, and films about belonging.

## Method and evidence

Searches covered official brand YouTube results, production-company/director/editor Vimeo pages, brand press/media portals, credited creative portfolios, and selected campaign archives. Query families included IKEA Place/Kreativ; home personality; Pinterest identity-to-action; furniture startups; Airbnb belonging; Sonos/music-at-home; personalized lighting; AR furniture; Modsy/Houzz. Instagram and X were not independently crawled in this sub-research.

- **Confirmed** means the cited page or indexed result establishes that title, uploader, brief, or metadata. It does not mean I have visually verified the film.
- **Inferred** means the candidate's potential lesson for ReSpace. All relevance and ranking judgments below are inferences from the available source descriptions.
- YouTube views are **indexed snapshots**, not a synchronized live leaderboard; capture date is above. Historical numbers are explicitly marked. Paid distribution versus organic sharing is unknown. Views do not establish that an editing style caused success.
- A functioning text page is not proof that playback works in every region. Several YouTube direct opens returned consent shells or fetch errors while search indexing exposed metadata.
- Related uploads and language/cutdown duplicates count once. The six IKEA Snap Changes entries are separately titled films with different subjects on one creator page, not six reposts of one film.
- The parent research handles the requested youtube-full setup. This pass used web sources because the TranscriptOut key was not available to this agent.

## Ten strongest candidates for the later deep-watch stage

This ranking prioritizes relevance and creative transferability, not a claim of highest global views.

1. **[Disco Treehouse — It’s Possible — Pinterest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hehaJGC9NJQ)** (S016, Pinterest). Closest identity-to-home match: a couple combines different tastes into a shared environment. The official description and credited creator case establish the concept; inspect the actual hook and product handoff next.

2. **[HomePod — Welcome Home [portfolio title: apple_home pod — dir. spike jonze]](https://vimeo.com/821121634)** (S041, Apple). Most useful cinematic ambition reference for treating an interior as an expression of its occupant's emotional state. Preview the film before borrowing pacing; the available URL is a portfolio upload.

3. **[IKEA Kreativ - Home Office](https://vimeo.com/926031195)** (S003, IKEA). Closest workflow match to ReSpace: existing room, scan, virtual clearing, redesign. A production-company case confirms a composed live-action/motion treatment rather than leaving only an app-demo reference.

4. **[IKEA Make Room for Life 2018](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSKtiEA8qMw)** (S007, IKEA). Best explicit big-picture source language about rooms carrying needs, dreams and daily life. Study how the abstract premise becomes particular activities and products.

5. **[IKEA - Ghosts](https://vimeo.com/265153539)** (S005, IKEA). Directly addresses choosing a room that reflects personality and fear of standing out. Excellent hypothesis for a relatable opening; the detailed premise is currently supported by archived campaign copy.

6. **[Say Hej to IKEA Place](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UudV1VdFtuQ)** (S001, IKEA). Essential category benchmark and an original AR furniture app launch. Use it to set the minimum clarity needed when the video transitions from worldview to actual functionality.

7. **[SONOS: Music Makes It Home](https://vimeo.com/156330951)** (S043, Sonos / Apple Music). Production company frames the film around a general question about how home relationships change with shared music. Useful model for making the product the answer to a human question.

8. **[Tylko – Spaces – Bedroom](https://vimeo.com/705647639)** (S023, Tylko). The creator states that the brief centers on a whole personal space rather than one furniture object. Good candidate for visually integrating ReSpace's furniture choices into a coherent room.

9. **[Introducing Icons](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz2FWiJq4Mw)** (S032, Airbnb). A genuine product-category launch that promises access to imagined worlds. Useful for a professional reveal and aspirational scale; avoid assuming its celebrity production translates to startup reach.

10. **[The Floyd Ethos](https://vimeo.com/359619840)** (S020, Floyd). A furniture startup manifesto grounded in founders and a real city. Good source for product philosophy and credible professional brand tone.

Useful alternates: **S008 Home Is A Different World** for a direct home manifesto; **S024 Mariposa Family** for a single-space product story; **S033 Stay Together for Less** for a high-view indexed spatial benefit; **S052 Silence the Critics** for home-related insecurity (obtain the full master).

## What the shortlist should answer next

For each finalist, inspect the actual film and record: opening image/line; first product or brand appearance; premise-to-product transition; recurring visual device; shot lengths and changes in pace; relationship of music, sound effects and speech; proof of real usefulness; emotional payoff; ending/CTA; production techniques we can reproduce with the team's time and assets. Keep a source timestamp for every claimed editing observation.

The most promising direction from this spatial subset is **a recognizable human tension around who you are versus how your room feels, followed by an observable room transformation and a clear scan-to-design proof**. That is a creative hypothesis from these references, not a scientific claim that a room determines personality or a guarantee of virality.
