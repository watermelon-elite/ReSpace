## Strongest ReSpace transfers — creative inference

1. **Keep each proof beat causal.** Use actual input → actual response, as in **00:12.500–00:21.000**. For ReSpace this can be an intention or a supported edit followed by the affected area in the proposal. It should be one understandable objective in one room rather than a montage of unrelated AI abilities.
2. **Make the visual system adapt to the content.** A wider room view establishes geography; a closer actual interface view makes a choice readable; a matching room view lets the viewer inspect the change. This borrows changes in scale and density without reproducing Notion's mascot or color palette.
3. **Let titles clear space between demonstrations.** A brief benefit phrase can replace several sentences of explanation. Keep any text large enough to read in a phone feed, and leave the proposal on screen long enough for the relevant spatial relationship to register.
4. **Let the narration rest during important evidence.** A human opening can use voice, but the proof section can briefly rely on visuals and sound. This is a proposed adaptation of a film that itself has no narration, not a claim about its spoken script.

The affordable version is carefully directed capture footage, a small set of graphic transitions and two or three actual interactions. Reproducing extensive desktop UI choreography or inventing a new character is unnecessary for this story.
