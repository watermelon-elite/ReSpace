# Preserve actual furniture and wall decor as separate scene items

The user requires the actual captured furniture, posters, paintings, and whiteboards to appear in the model. Preserve observed shape and appearance in independent assets rather than silently substituting similar catalog models or flattening wall decor into the wallpaper. This makes multi-view ownership, source-faithful textures and wall-support relationships part of the scene contract; generated hidden completion may fill missing surfaces but must not rewrite visible artwork, board content, or distinctive furniture details. Lighting simulation, relighting and shadow cleanup are outside scope, so they are not prerequisites for publishing these assets.
