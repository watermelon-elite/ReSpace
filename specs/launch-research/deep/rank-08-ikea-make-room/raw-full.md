## 9. Failure Modes and Cautionary Notes for ReSpace

- **The "Vibe Commercial" Trap Without Functional Proof:** IKEA can afford an 89-second brand film that delays showing corporate identity until `01:21.000` because its global brand recognition is already universal. If an early-stage startup like ReSpace copies this impressionistic pacing, viewers will churn within 5 seconds without understanding what software product is being offered. ReSpace must establish its LiDAR scanning utility within the initial 3–5 seconds.
- **Misleading Transformation Expectations:** In IKEA’s film, rooms transform effortlessly between parties, gym spaces, and dance floors. If ReSpace’s marketing mirrors this too closely, users may expect the app to magically solve physical decluttering or provide instant physical delivery, leading to poor App Store reviews when they realize the app delivers a digital 3D layout plan.
- **The Photorealism Disconnect:** The cinematic warmth of 35mm-style cinematography, if juxtaposed carelessly against ReSpace's non-photoreal MVP 3D assets, could create an unflattering contrast. The commercial should embrace an intentional, stylized visual aesthetic that celebrates clean geometric 3D models rather than trying to disguise them as reality.
