## Strongest ReSpace applications — proposals

1. **Let the filmmaking change when the room begins to support the person.** A repetitive obstruction or crowded composition can give way to a deliberate camera move, a clear pathway and a simpler sound field. This can communicate a specific improvement before voiceover explains it. Calm and minimalism should not be treated as everyone’s desired personality; the after-state must reflect the character’s own life.
2. **Use a motif to cross from human story into product proof.** A line drawn by a chair leg, a doorway edge or a person tracing an awkward corner can match to the actual scanned geometry in ReSpace. That creates a motivated visual bridge while keeping the real app screen identifiable. It does not require a photoreal AR transformation.
3. **Make the product resolve the precise thesis.** If the story is about a room that no longer fits someone’s routine, show that routine, the scan, an intent choice, the proposed 3D layout and a real product decision. The larger claim then has a visible chain of support. A generic beautiful render would not close the argument.

**Small-team feasibility:** a designed before/after composition, controlled sound, one recurring geometric motif and real app inserts are practical. A two-minute animated dystopia, morphing faces, underwater imagery or large-scale architectural changes are not the necessary lesson and would raise costs sharply. Literal shrinking walls are optional stylization; simpler blocking can show the same spatial pressure.

**What not to copy:** the length of the delayed product reveal, the horror tone, or a generalized claim that technology or a room determines mental health. ReSpace’s film can make a human observation about environment without claiming a psychological diagnosis. Raw proposals involving instant clutter dissolution, guaranteed optimal arrangement, drag-and-drop controls or live AR are not established features and are not adopted.

## Implication for the launch direction

This is the strongest reference for an argument that earns a product reveal. It is a weaker template for the literal duration and production of a fast social launch. Borrow the causal relationship—problem, felt change, evidence—and compress it around one person and one room. That recommendation is a creative inference; the film does not reveal which portion drove its view count.
