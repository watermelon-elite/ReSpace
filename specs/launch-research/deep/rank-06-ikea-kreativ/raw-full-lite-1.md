## 9. Three specific transferable devices
1. **Rhythmic Object Swaps via Jump Cuts**
   - *Timestamp:* 00:00.000 – 00:01.333
   - *Scaled-down adaptation:* Use quick match-cuts of a single actor swapping props on a beat to show hobby overload in low-budget social ads.
   - *What not to copy:* Avoid excessive visual noise if the core product benefit is simplicity.
2. **Interactive UI Wireframe Overlays on Live Action**
   - *Timestamp:* 00:05.000 – 00:06.500
   - *Scaled-down adaptation:* Overlay simple 2D vector outlines on footage to highlight messy zones in home improvement or design content.
   - *What not to copy:* Do not overcomplicate UI animations to the point where the underlying physical product gets lost.
3. **Magical Before-and-After Match Cut**
   - *Timestamp:* 00:10.000 – 00:11.000
   - *Scaled-down adaptation:* Use a locked-off tripod shot with a simple dissolve or hard cut between messy and clean states to demonstrate transformation value.
