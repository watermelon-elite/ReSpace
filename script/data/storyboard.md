The current Working opening and closing are preserved. The middle replaces the entire passage from Meet ReSpace through the final sharing/diagram beat. Its twelve narration passages and source directions come from the approved `.lavish/respace-middle.html`; editable production layers are in `middle.json`.

The master remains 180 seconds in 16:9. Opening: before 00:24. Middle: 00:24–02:42. Closing: 02:42–03:00. The viewer is a script and production-planning screen, without video playback. Timeline widths follow words; exact layer times remain in the data. Overlay-free beauty hold: 01:57–02:01.

## Recording copy

This dorm room could belong to anyone.
Same bed. Same desk. Blank walls.
But you are not just "anyone" 

You are YOU

Your room should tell a story only you could tell

And give you room to grow

After all, a flower can only bloom in the right environment.

Meet ReSpace. Scan your room. LiDAR captures its shape and measurements, giving the design a real space to work with.

Choose what stays. Set a budget. Then tell it how you want to live here: somewhere to concentrate, with room for friends.

ReSpace asks questions that help shape the plan. When friends come over, where does everyone sit? And which of these spaces feels more like you?

Your answers build a taste profile: the colors and materials you like, and how you want the room to feel.

Research on how people use and experience spaces informs the plan, alongside your preferences. Room to work, move, and spend time together.

Gemini searches for real products and compares them against your room, preferences and budget. Product details are saved for reuse, with offer checks when needed.

Gemini plans the arrangement. A placement solver uses the measurements to position the furniture and check fit, collisions and clear paths.

Explore the layout in 3D, then see a photorealistic view of your room. Get a feel for it before buying anything.

Need more desk space? Ask for a smaller lamp. Compare the change. Undo it whenever you want.

Tap a piece for its size, price and where to buy it. Your shopping list keeps the products and links together.

Share the whole list. Even with Mom.

A café for conversation. An office for focused work. Share your design, and ask the people who’ll use it what they think.

It's still the room you walked into.
But now, you can see yourself here.
ReSpace.
Make room for you.
Join the waitlist.
