## Strongest ReSpace transfers

**Introduce the app through a decision someone would actually make.** A person looks at a corner, considers an activity or identifies an object worth keeping; the scan follows from that need. This is more coherent than cutting from a philosophical introduction to an unrelated phone recording.

**Show the setup as a small human ritual.** A deliberate phone movement, the actual room capture and one meaningful intention can form the bridge into the proposal. Preserve the same room so the result can be assessed against the opening.

**End with an activity, not an unattended render.** Let the viewer understand what the proposed arrangement makes possible. If a physical after-scene is staged, distinguish it from the app's 3D output and processing behavior. A human return can be modest: sitting in a newly useful corner or welcoming someone into the room.

The economical adaptation needs one room and one person or pair, rather than multiple households or recognizable performers. The original has only a brief operational sequence; ReSpace will need clearer scan → intention → proposal evidence because its unfamiliar workflow cannot be inferred from music playing. No claim about psychological improvement, family relationships or conversion is established by this film.
